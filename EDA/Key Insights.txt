## Key Insights

Based on the exploratory data analysis of the Olist E-commerce dataset, the following insights were identified:

1. Order Volume: The dataset contains 99,441 orders, with 96,478 orders marked as delivered, representing approximately 97.02% of all order records.

2. Order Patterns: Monday recorded the highest order volume with 16,196 orders, while Saturday recorded the lowest with 10,887 orders.

3. Data Quality: No exact duplicate rows or duplicate order IDs were identified. However, missing timestamps require attention before further analysis.

4. Delivery Data: A total of 2,965 orders have missing customer delivery dates. Eight of these orders are marked as delivered, indicating a need for further investigation.

5. Monthly Trends: Monthly order analysis provides a way to identify changes in demand and potential seasonal shopping patterns.

6. Delivery Performance: Comparing actual and estimated delivery times helps evaluate delivery efficiency and identify potential delays.

### Business Recommendations

- Investigate missing delivery timestamps and improve order tracking.
- Use monthly and weekday order trends for operational planning.
- Monitor cancellations and unavailable orders.
- Extend the analysis to payments, products, customers, sellers, and reviews to generate additional business insights.

### Data Visualizations

1. Number of Orders by Status: Visualizes the distribution of orders across different statuses and highlights overall order fulfillment patterns.

2. Monthly Order Trends: Shows monthly changes in order volume to help identify demand patterns and potential seasonal trends.

3. Orders by Day of the Week: Compares daily order volumes and identifies Monday as the busiest day and Saturday as the least busy day in the dataset.

4. Actual vs. Estimated Delivery Time: Compares actual delivery durations with estimated delivery durations to evaluate delivery performance and identify potential delays.
